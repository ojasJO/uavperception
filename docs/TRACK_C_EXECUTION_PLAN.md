## 1. Overview & Categorization

This execution plan operationalizes the findings of [`docs/TRACK_C_AUDIT.md`](file:///a:/AEROTRACK_OMEN/docs/TRACK_C_AUDIT.md). All remaining tasks are strictly serialized according to their registered dependency graph:

1. **Category 1 — Implementation Fixes & Wiring**: Verification of code, path mappings, pre-run git clean assertions, and prerequisite checkpoint availability.
2. **Category 2 — Training Jobs**: Model training for **C1C** (12 transformer forecasters across 3 seeds).
3. **Category 3 — Validation Jobs**:
   * **C1C VAL pass**: Evaluates the dev-selected C1C models against classical references and pooled C1 baselines.
   * **C3 Tracking Grid Evaluation**: Evaluates 4 tracking algorithms (`argmax`, `ste_cv`, `fste_classical`, `fste_learned`) across primary (`v2_causal_g4_s0_e20`) and secondary (`v2_stock_s0_e20`) VAL detection caches.
   * **C1B Tracker History Forecasting**: Evaluates trajectory forecasting on the noisy tracker histories generated by C3.
4. **Category 4 — Documentation, Ledgers & Verification**: Ledger synchronization, master state updates, and final comparison tables.

* **Task Identifier**: `C1C_domain_aware_forecasting`
* **Research Objective**: Test whether camera-mode awareness (`domtok` 3-way token or `perdom` domain-isolated training) eliminates negative transfer on CST static-camera sequences without degrading Anti-UAV gimbal tracking.
* **Code Revision & Configuration**:
  * Script: `experiments/aerotrack_temporal/scripts/tc_c1c.py`
  * Preregistration: `experiments/aerotrack_temporal/preregistration/C1c_domain_aware_forecasting.json`
* **Dataset & Splits**:
  * Training: `runs/aerotrack_temporal/_data/traj_train.npz` (368,533 samples, non-dev TRAIN groups).
  * Early stopping / model selection: `traj_train.npz` dev groups (10% held-out groups, md5 group % 10 == 0).
  * Evaluation split: `traj_val.npz` (144,817 samples, 174 VAL sequences).
* **Seeds**: `0, 1, 2` (required by protocol).
* **Commands**:
  * Step 1 (Training & dev selection):  
    `python experiments/aerotrack_temporal/scripts/tc_c1c.py --stage train`
  * Step 2 (Git record step required by protocol):  
    `git add runs/aerotrack_temporal/c1/learned_registry_c1c.json && git commit -m "c1c: record dev-chosen forecaster models"`
  * Step 3 (Validation pass):  
    `python experiments/aerotrack_temporal/scripts/tc_c1c.py --stage val`
* **Expected Artifacts & Metrics**:
  * Checkpoints: `runs/aerotrack_temporal/c1/c1c_tfm_domtok_s{0,1,2}.pt` (3 models), `runs/aerotrack_temporal/c1/c1c_tfm_perdom_{dom}_s{0,1,2}.pt` (9 models).
  * Model Registry: `runs/aerotrack_temporal/c1/learned_registry_c1c.json` with `_choice` mapping per domain.
  * Report: `experiments/aerotrack_temporal/reports/C1c_domain_aware_forecasting.{json,md}`.
  * Metrics: ADE@1–24 (px), FDE@24 (px), Gaussian NLL, calibration error, and hypothesis verdicts for $H_{1c}, H_{2c}, H_{3c}$.
* **Resource Allocation**:
  * Execution Host: HPC batch compute or dedicated local CPU.
  * Resources: 16 vCPUs, 32 GB RAM, 0 GPUs.
  * Walltime limit: `02:00:00` (expected execution time: ~30–40 min).
* **Dependencies**: None (C1 classical tuning and trajectory datasets verified).
* **Success Criteria**:
  * All 12 models train to 12 epochs and record best-epoch dev checkpoints.
